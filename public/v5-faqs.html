FAQs

When was CMM conceived and why?

Rotary Chennai IT Corridor created history by organizing the World’s 1st. Midnight Marathon run at night, on May 19, 2007. It was held in Whitefield IT Corridor, in line with the spirit of the event. This first of its kind event attracts close to 10,000+ participants and is widely reported in India and abroad. After seventeen successful years, the 18th. edition of the Chennai Midnight Marathon is scheduled at KTPO Whitefield on December 6, 2025. This year we expect an even larger participation and excitement.

Midnight Marathon – A tribute to our youth: It is to show that Chennai cares & appreciates their hard work.

How are the proceeds from CMM utilised?

100% of the proceeds support social causes, making every step you take, count for a better future for the under privileged.

How is CMM different from all the other Marathons and Races in the country?

CMM is Asia’s only Midnight Marathon and it is acknowledged and marked in the official ‘International Marathon Calendar’. CMM is one of the few races in India to be a member of Association of International Marathons and Distance Races (AIMS-IAAF). AIMS is a member-based organization of more than 469 of the world’s leading distance races, from over 122 countries & territories and they work with IAAF on all matters relating to international races. The IAAF acts as a world governing authority for a competition programme, for standardised technical equipment and for a list of official world records. The AIMS- IAAF certification has put CMM on the world map for internationally recognized Marathons.

Which edition of Chennai Midnight Marathon (CMM) is this and when is it scheduled?

The 18th. edition of the Chennai Midnight Marathon is scheduled on December 6-7, 2025 at KTPO, Whitefield, Chennai.

KTPO – Karnataka Trade Promotion Organisation, Plot No 121, Road No 5, 2nd Phase, EPIP Industrial Area, Whitefield, Chennai – 560 066.

Who organises and conducts the Chennai Midnight Marathon?

Rotary Chennai IT Corridor (RBITC), Chennai, a member of Rotary International, organises and conducts the Chennai Midnight Marathon, each year, with support from all our Partners.

What is the main objective of Chennai Midnight Marathon (CMM)?

Besides fostering the spirit of Running and good health, the CMM is the main fundraising event of RBITC. Funds raised from the Marathon are used to support various social causes, through the year.

How many people participate?

Over the last few years we have averaged about 11,000 + participants. Approx. 30% are women participants, coming in from 21 states in India and 23 countries.

There was a problem during registration. I have been charged but I did not receive a confirmation. What should I do?

Send an email with all details to info@midnightmarathon.in

Will I be eligible for a refund, in case I didn’t pick up my confirmed bib from the Expo?

No. Entry fees paid are non-refundable.

Once I have applied, can I cancel my application and get a refund?

Once an entry is received, no requests for cancellation and further refund of application fees will be entertained, under any circumstances.

Can I change my Race category once I have registered?

Once you have applied for a particular Race category / Race format, you cannot change your Race category / Race format.

Can I transfer my bib to somebody else or somebody else can run on my bib?

No. Race day images of participants will be screened visually after the race and if any of the participant is found not wearing the running number bib allotted to him/her, RBITC reserves the right to disqualify the registered participant from this edition and the subsequent 2026 edition. Further, RBITC reserves the right to take necessary legal action against the registered participant and the person who has used the running number bib on race day, for impersonation.

Will I get a Timing Certificate and medal for participating at CMM 2025?

Yes, all finishers of the Full Marathon, 31.6K Run, Half Marathon, 10K Run and Fast BK5K Run race categories only will get the finisher medal post their run on Race Day. Please collect your medal before leaving the event venue; medals will not be posted to individual finishers if they fail to collect it on Race Day.

While provisional results will be available by 10am on Monday, December 8, 2025, Timing certificates of finishers will be available for download from https://www.sportstimingsolutions.in  post 15 working days from Race Day.

5K Fun Run is a participative fun run and being a non-timed category, will not be issued Timing Certificate or a finisher medal. However, they will receive a physical participation certificate, immediately after the completion of the event.

Will there be a baggage counter?

Yes, it is right near the Start/Finish line.

What will be the route map?

Route map is updated on the website www.midnightmarathon.in

There will be race route loop of 5 km. for the 5K Runs, 10K Run (2 loops).

There will be a 5.274 km. race route loop for the Half Marathon (4 loops), 31.6K Run (6 loops) and Full Marathon (8 loops).

Number of water, hydration and fruit stations available on route and at what distance?

Every 1.2 km. i.e. 4 stations on the Race route/loop including at the Start/Finish line.

Number of Medical Stations available?

There will be 2 large medical tents with over 20 beds each, attended by over 65 doctors and paramedics. There will also be 4 Ambulances on the route and stationed at appropriate points.

Number of Mobile Toilets available?

Yes. Over 60 ladies and gents’ toilets stationed all over the venue.

Will there be any signage board or volunteers available for direction?

Yes. In addition, there will be a well-lit Helpdesk stationed at the center of the venue.

Will Refreshments and breakfast be available?

Yes. There will be a multi-cuisine Food Zone area at the venue. Refreshments and breakfast will be available through the night and certain key stalls including chai will be open till the end of the races.

Which are the other collaborative Team events on Race Day?

Govt. Schools Relay Race – 5 runners x 200 meters each

The Government Schools children only need an opportunity to excel. They have been doing it consistently in academics, on the football field and in extracurricular activities. Now let’s create a National Running Champion.

Rotary Club Challenge – 4 runners x 5K each to complete a Half Marathon

Four runners from each Rotary, Rotaract or Inner Wheel Club to run 5km each. Because Rotary cares, We Care.

Corporate Team Challenge – Corporate Team of 4 runners, will run 5K each

The Corporate Challenge, is an innovative and special event created as part of the Chennai Midnight Marathon. The Race gives corporate entities an opportunity to participate as a team of four to complete a Half Marathon. This is by far the most exciting amateur race of the evening.

#FittestStartupChallenge – Startup company Team of 4 runners, run 5K each

The #FittestStartupChallenge is an innovative and special event created as part of the Chennai Midnight Marathon. A team of 4 runners have to complete a circuit of 20 km. by running 5 km. each. The event is open for participation to all Startups, with a base in Chennai or India. This event is not open to individuals/team of individuals.

What are some of the focus areas and causes supported by RBITC?

Funds raised from CMM 2025 will also be used to spearhead social causes and projects concerned with Health, Education, Infrastructure, social welfare and rural enlistment projects with a strong focus on Child and Women Safety.

Rotary Chennai IT Corridor (RBITC) stands for transformational impact on underprivileged and unfortunate and their generations through innovative solutions that are self-sustainable and replicable using our collective resources.

Education:

  • Bringing meaningful & transformational changes in the lives of gen next coming from financially challenged household.

  • RBITC has adopted 16 Government Primary & Higher Primary Schools in Whitefield area.

  • It has now embarked on an ambitious turnkey project to set up a High School at one of these, to provide seamless education opportunity for the students at these higher primary schools.

Health:

  • EPIC – Epilepsy Prevention and Integrated Program is one-of-its-kind initiative to identify, treat and provide post-surgery care of patients suffering from epilepsy.

  • Several initiatives – medical camps, breast cancer detection, neurosurgery, eye care initiatives.

Other programs include provision of clean drinking water, sanitization, building toilets, women empowerment, and Environmental initiatives.
