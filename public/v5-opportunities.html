  • Company Official/Ambassador helps to start the event. Opportunity to speak/present an award at the Awards Ceremony
  • Leverage CMM to highlight company CSR initiatives. Run for a cause.
  • Branding and participation at pre and post-Race Day events
    • Pet-a-thon

    • Pasta Party
    • Thank you party

Other branding and visibility ideas can be discussed with Major Sponsors specifically if required.

Title And Race Event Partnerships

Title Partner

Brand integrated with Chennai Midnight Marathon (CMM) logo across all collaterals, media, campaigns, events, digital marketing and on-site branding. VIP Lounge. Training programs.

Associate Title Partner

Brand attached to the CMM logo, as ‘Powered by…’ across all collaterals, media, campaigns, events, digital marketing and on site branding. Training programs.

5K Fun Run Partner

The 5K IT City Fun Run is the flagship event of CMM. It is the event that has the maximum participation with thousands turning up for it.  This is an event for people who aren’t professional or serious long-distance runners and yet they love running and want to participate in a smaller 5 km stretch along with friends, families and pets. At CMM, the 5K is run as a single loop.

Fast 5K Run (Timed) Partner

Running a 5K is a great way to challenge yourself if you’re already a Runner, or to set a goal for yourself if you’re starting to run for the first time. Pace yourself as you build your speed, endurance, and strength, but also be sure to challenge yourself along the way. At CMM, the Fast 5K is run as a single loop.

10K Run Partner

Distance: 10 KM (2 Loops)

The 10K Run is the T20 Cricket of Marathons across the world. Immensely popular, this event has taken the world by storm. This is an event for people who aren’t professionals but are serious long-distance runners and yet to come to terms with a full Marathon. As one of the shortest common road distances, many 10K races attract high levels of public participation. At CMM, the Race route is of 5 km loop. Hence the 10K is run over 2 loops.

Half Marathon Partner

Distance: 21.097 KM (4 loops of 5.27 km each)

A Half Marathon is a road running event of 21.0975 km. (13 miles 192.5 yards)—half the distance of a marathon. It is common for a half marathon event to be held concurrently with a marathon or a 5K race, using almost the same course with a late start, an early finish or shortcuts. A half marathon world record is officially recognised by the International Association of Athletics Federations. Participation in half marathons has grown steadily since 2003, partly because it is a challenging distance, but does not require the same level of training that a marathon does. At CMM, the Race route is made up of 4 loops, each of distance 5.274 km.

31.6K Run Partner

Distance: 31.65 KM (6 loops of 5.27 km each) – NEW

If you can run a Half, you are well on your way to being able to run 42.195 km.

The 31.6K Run is a road running event of 31.65 km.  This all new entrant distance has taken the worlds fancy as  the  stepping-stone towards running a Full Marathon.

Full Marathon Partner

Distance: 42.195 KM (8 Loops of 5.27 km. each)

The Marathon is a long-distance foot race with a distance of 42.195 kilometres (26 miles 385 yards), usually run as a road race, but the distance can be covered on trail routes. The marathon can be completed by running or with a run/walk strategy.

Annually, more than 900 marathons are organised worldwide. Some of these, like Chennai Midnight Marathon are members of Association of International Marathons and Distance Races (AIMS) which has grown since its foundation in 1982 to embrace over 446 member events in 111 countries and territories. At CMM, the Race route is made up of 8 loops, each, of distance 5.274 km.

Corporate Team Challenge Partner

Distance: 4 Runners x 5 KM each

A team of 4 individual runners have to complete a circuit of 20 km. by running 5 km. each. The team totalling the least time wins the Cup. 

This event provides an amazing opportunity for these entities to contribute to Corporate Social Responsibilities, align with their employee retention programmes and provide brand building initiatives.

Team gets to participate in a Special Race, which is both  competitive and fun in nature, gets branding on Race day and exclusive space to put up promotional material.

#FittestStartupChallenge Partner

Distance: 4 Runners x 5 KM each

The #FittestStartupChallenge Relay is an innovative and special event created as part of the Chennai Midnight Marathon. A team of 4 runners have to complete a circuit of 20 km. by running 5 km. each. The team totalling the least time wins the Cup.

The event is open for participation to all startups, with a base in Chennai or India. This event is not open to individuals/team of individuals.

Community Relay Partner

The Community Relay is another exciting and involved team event created to involve communities of all age groups, in and around the Whitefield area in running. The relay gives Community entities an opportunity to be part of the fun, frolic and encourages competition at the Chennai Midnight Marathon. As the name suggests, it is a relay, run by a team of five people in various age groups to run a distance of 500m. each. A community may enter up to 4 teams.

Govt. Schools Relay Partner

Distance: 5 Runners X 100 M Each

The Government schools children only need an opportunity to excel. They have been doing it consistently in academics, on the football field and in extracurricular activities. Now let’s create a National Running Champion.

Associate Partners

Associate Partners either bring cash to the table or a service. Ideally, there should be a cash component involved even though they offer a free product/service.

Entitlements: Branding across print & outdoor media and at-venue branding. Branding in poster campaigns, at corporate campuses and ground activation. Social media and digital marketing opportunities.

Examples of Associate Partners would be:

Hospitality Partner

Typically, a Star Hotel provide ‘in kind’ Banqueting space, food, and stay for Athletes/Dignitaries. They host the Press Conference, pre-race Pasta Party, and Post Race Thank you Party.

Lead Car Partner

In front of the Marathon, Half Marathon and 10k Run, there is always a Lead car. This car can be partnered with a car manufacturer/distributor. The Car becomes the Lead car and is branded with the Manufacturer’s message. A prime display space is also provided at the venue.

Healthcare Partner

Considering the grueling Marathon and other races, critical Medical facilities are required at the venue, and on the race route. We need 4-5 Ambulances with trained Nurses, Doctors and emergency care equipment on route and a medical tent with beds, trained Nurses and Doctors at the venue. This is a service and typically comes with a cash component.

Banking Partner

Banking Partner typically facilitates the financial transactions involved, For example a separate account for the event, Online Credit Card payment gateway, etc.

Timing Partner

The timing partner should typically be a watch manufacturer. They provide us with big clocks for timing of the race. Typically, four of them. Two on lead vehicles of the race and two at the venue. This service though not necessary, does add spice to the event.

Energy Partner

Typically, a product partner. They provided the runners with free beverages. These products should not in any way be adverse to health.

Hydration Partner

Water is essential to a race and large quantities are consumed. We have to provide water to all participants on Race day and other events.

Technology Partner

Technology partner can provide, registration facilitation services, Call Center services and cover the cost of the RFID based timing chips used by marathoners and half marathoners.

Entertainment Partner

Each year, we have a Rock Band/Musical Performance at the event. We also showcase traditional Karnataka performances, dholaks etc. on the route. The Entertainment partner will be expected to fit the bill of the show and performers.

Apparel Partner

Apparel partner is expected to provide us with t-shirts for all the Runners, officials, and volunteers at the race. Typically, this will be around 7,500 people. They can sell official merchandise through their outlets. They are also expected to pay for the association.

Lighting Partner

Since the races are being held during the hours of the night, we will be using extensive lighting. A lighting partner would be expected to foot the bill for the lighting.

Shoes

Since the race is a running race this is a great opportunity for sports shoes makers to associate with the event and gain mileage. Typically, the outlets of these stores help promote the race.

Pre And Post Race Events Partnership

CXO Power Mile Partnership

A Special pre-race day event set up especially for CEO’s/CXOs of large IT and other companies. Last few years, more than 150 of them have participated in this event. It starts with light entertainment, continued by a 1 Mile walk/run and followed by cocktails and lunch. It’s a great opportunity for targeting corporate entities CEO’s/CXOs for partnerships and participation. Also a great PR Opportunity.

Entitlements: Brand integrated event, branding at venue/route plus access to HNI

Pre-Race Pasta Party Partnership

Traditional pasta party/carbohydrate loading party traditionally held for athletes but are now a social affair attended primarily by international athletes, Media, partners, Page 3 people and officials.

Entitlements: Branding and press release on same. Plus branding across print & outdoor media and at venue branding. Branding in poster campaign at corporate campuses and ground activation.

Pet-A-Thon Partnership

When there is so much excitement and running, why should our pets be left behind. The Pet-a-thon is conducted as a run up to the Marathon. Games are conducted for the pets and lots of goodies and prizes to be won in various events

Post-Race Awards And Thank You Party Partnership

A party to celebrate the marathon task of conducting a Marathon. It is primarily a social affair attended by international athletes, Media, partners, Page 3 people and officials and volunteers.
